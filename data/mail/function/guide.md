# Mailbox Guide

Receiving: Rename and place a barrel with a unique 3-32 character ID using lowercase letters, numbers, or underscores, such as `xav_house1`. Invalid or duplicate IDs stay ordinary barrels. A valid named bundle can reserve its ID before the matching receiving barrel is placed.

Sending Mailbox

1. Rename a barrel to `Mailbox` and place it.
2. Rename a bundle or shulker box to the recipient ID.
3. Add items, put it in the sender, and close it.

Bundles send contents one stack at a time and remain as synchronized reusable addresses. Shulker boxes send whole and do not sync. Missing or full receivers leave mail in the sender.

Remove an address bundle from any open sending mailbox to delete that address from every sender and disable delivery. Everyone receives an actionbar notice. To restore it, keep the mailbox open and insert a bundle with that name again. Mailboxes have no owners.
